Title: Comprehensive Paris Travel & Cultural Guide
City: Paris, France
Tags: history, museum, photography, art, dining, transport, tips

Overview:
Paris, the City of Light (La Ville Lumière), is organized into 20 numbered arrondissements spiraling clockwise from the Seine River. The city blends world-class art collections, architectural splendor, café culture, and culinary excellence.

Key Cultural Etiquette & Local Rules:
- Always greet shopkeepers and waiters with 'Bonjour Madame/Monsieur' when entering an establishment and 'Merci, au revoir' upon leaving. Skipping this is considered impolite.
- Tipping: A service charge (service compris) of 15% is legally included in French restaurant bills. Rounding up to the nearest Euro or leaving 5-10% in cash for exceptional service is customary but not obligatory.
- Metro & Public Transit: Paris has an extensive Metro and RER train system. Keep your validated ticket until you exit the station, as random ticket inspections by controllers incur steep on-the-spot fines (€35-€50). Navigo Easy card or digital phone passes offer discounted 10-ticket carnets.

Attractions & Timings:
- The Louvre Museum requires timed-entry tickets reserved in advance online. The museum is closed on Tuesdays. Peak hours are 11:00 AM to 3:00 PM; Friday evening openings until 9:45 PM have fewer crowds.
- The Eiffel Tower ticket options include stairs or elevators up to the summit (276m). Illuminations sparkle for 5 minutes at the beginning of each hour after sunset until 1:00 AM.
- Musée d'Orsay houses the premier collection of Impressionism and Post-Impressionism (Monet, Van Gogh, Renoir, Degas). Closed on Mondays.
- Sainte-Chapelle is famed for its 1,113 stained glass windows dating to the 13th century. It is located on the Île de la Cité; combined tickets with the Conciergerie are available.

Best Photo Spots:
- Trocadéro Gardens & Place du Trocadéro for sunrise framing of the Eiffel Tower without crowds.
- Rue de l'Université and Rue de Buenos-Aires for dramatic ground-up perspectives of the Eiffel Tower.
- Pont Alexandre III with ornate golden cherubs and views of the Grand Palais and Invalides.
- Montmartre's Rue de l'Abreuvoir and Place du Tertre for picturesque cobblestone village charm.

Budget & Food Tips:
- Bakeries (boulangeries) provide fresh baguettes, quiches, and croissants for under €2-€5 for lunch.
- Try traditional bouillons (such as Bouillon Chartier or Bouillon Pigalle) for hearty three-course classic French meals under €20.
- Tap water in Paris is completely safe and free; ask for 'une carafe d'eau' at any restaurant.
