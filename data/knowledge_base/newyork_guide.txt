Title: Comprehensive New York City Travel Guide
City: New York, United States
Tags: skyline, broadway, museums, shopping, food, parks, photography

Overview:
New York City is a global capital of finance, culture, entertainment, and gastronomy spanning five boroughs: Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx, and Staten Island.

Key Local Tips & Etiquette:
- Tipping: Standard gratuity in NYC sit-down restaurants is 18% to 22% of the pre-tax bill. Bartenders receive $1-$2 per drink; taxi drivers expect 15-20%.
- Subway Navigation: The NYC Subway runs 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Use OMNY contactless payment (tap credit card, Apple Pay, or Google Pay) at the turnstile without buying a paper MetroCard. A 7-day fare cap automatically applies after 12 paid rides.
- Pedestrian Flow: New Yorkers walk briskly with intent. Avoid stopping abruptly in the middle of busy sidewalks or station stairs; step to the side near building edges to check maps or take photos.

Attractions & Timings:
- The Metropolitan Museum of Art (The Met): One of the world's most comprehensive museums; general admission covers access to all galleries and rooftop garden with Central Park vistas.
- Central Park: Stretches from 59th to 110th Street. Highlights include Bethesda Terrace, Bow Bridge, the Ramble, and Sheep Meadow. Free audio guides and maps are available at visitor kiosks.
- Broadway Shows: Discount same-day tickets (20-50% off) can be purchased at the TKTS booth in Times Square or Lincoln Center.
- Observation Decks: Top of the Rock provides unobstructed views of the Empire State Building and Central Park; Edge at Hudson Yards offers outdoor cantilevered glass floors; Summit One Vanderbilt provides reflective infinity rooms.
