Title: Global Travel Rules, Visa Essentials & Smart Budgeting Methodology
Tags: budget, currency, packing, visa, safety, flights, planning

Universal Travel Planning Rules:
1. Pacing & Feasibility: An optimal urban sightseeing day accommodates 3 to 4 major attractions maximum. Attempting more than 4 sites causes traveler fatigue, delays, and diminishes cultural appreciation.
2. Distance & Proximity Routing: Group morning, afternoon, and evening sights geographically within walking distance or a short direct metro hop. Avoid crisscrossing opposite sides of a metropolitan area in a single day.
3. Weather Contingency: Always have an indoor backup (museums, indoor markets, covered arcades, aquariums) for days forecasting heavy rain (precipitation probability > 50%) or extreme temperature spikes.

Budget Allocation Model (Recommended Industry Standard):
- International / Intercity Transport & Flights: ~35% - 40% of total travel budget
- Accommodation / Hotels: ~25% - 30% of total budget
- Food & Dining: ~15% - 20% of total budget
- Sightseeing & Activities (Attractions, tours, entry fees): ~8% - 12% of total budget
- Local City Transit (Metro passes, rideshare, taxis): ~5% of total budget
- Miscellaneous & Emergency Contingency: ~5% buffer

Currency Conversion Rules:
- When using credit cards abroad, always choose to be charged in the LOCAL destination currency (EUR, JPY, GBP) rather than your home currency. Choosing Dynamic Currency Conversion (DCC) charges inflated conversion markups (3% to 7%).
- Avoid airport cash exchange kiosks due to high commission spreads; use bank ATM machines within the destination city using a debit card with low international transaction fees.
